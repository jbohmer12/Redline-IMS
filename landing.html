Questions

Frequently Asked

Do I need to replace my existing DMS? +
No. Redline IMS works alongside your existing Dealer Management System — Lightspeed, DX1, Blackpurl, and others. Import your parts list via CSV and Redline handles the eBay side. Think of it as the eBay profit center your DMS doesn't have.
How accurate is the AI part identification? +
It uses your OEM part number to look up make, model, year, fitment, and description. For Honda, Yamaha, Kawasaki, Suzuki, Ducati, KTM, and Harley-Davidson numbers it's highly accurate. Obscure or regional parts get a best-guess that you can edit before saving.
What is a Data Logger seat? +
Data Loggers are staff-level accounts — warehouse workers, shipping staff, or interns who need to add and scan parts but shouldn't be able to delete records, change prices, or push eBay listings. Each seat is $19/month added to any plan.
Do I need my own eBay seller account? +
Yes. Redline IMS connects to your existing eBay Motors seller account via OAuth — you authorize it once and it lists on your behalf. You keep full control of your eBay store and can disconnect at any time.
Can I export my data if I want to leave? +
Yes. Full CSV export is available on all plans. Enterprise includes bulk data export and API access. Your data is always yours — no lock-in, no deletion on cancel.
What happens after the 14-day trial? +
You choose a plan and enter payment. If you don't, your account pauses — your data is kept safely for 30 days. No charges without action from you.
